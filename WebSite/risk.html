Our team created a risk register that included a description of each risk, its category, the probability of occurrence, the severity of its consequences, a mitigation strategy, and the person responsible. Risk mitigation measures included the use of backups, the appointment of a lead and a deputy for each area, regular testing, and maintaining communication with stakeholders.

Risks were monitored throughout the entire development process: our team regularly reviewed the register and updated assessments as needed. This helped our team identify potential issues early, mitigate their impact, and ensure the project proceeded according to plan.
