We have identified the third-party libraries, tools, and resources used to create our game, along with their respective licenses. We selected libraries such as libGDX based on licenses that permit commercial use and modification. We also utilized AI-generated assets that were subsequently edited by hand, while keeping a record of the prompts used. We have identified a few remaining licensing issues, specifically regarding LGPL-licensed audio libraries and the libGDX logo that must be resolved prior to a commercial release.
