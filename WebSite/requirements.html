The team gathered requirements by reviewing the product description, identifying unclear points, and discussing them with the stakeholder. Subsequently, the requirements were verified, and any that were contradictory or unfeasible were removed.

The requirements were organized into categories: user requirements, functional requirements, and quality requirements. Each requirement has a unique identifier and description, as well as defined priorities and links to other related requirements, ensuring clarity and traceability. The quality requirements also include measurable compliance criteria, which specify exactly how the game’s performance and usability will be evaluated. To learn more about the requirements, please refer to the attached PDF document.
